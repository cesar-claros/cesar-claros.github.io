I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the [University of Delaware](https://www.ece.udel.edu/), where I work with [Austin J. Brockmeier](https://www.eecis.udel.edu/~ajbrock/) in the Computational Neural and Information Engineering Lab.

My research is on reliable machine learning under distribution shift: out-of-distribution detection, calibration, and methods for interpreting where two datasets differ. I also apply machine learning to biomedical data, including brain age estimation from magnetic resonance elastography with [Curtis Johnson](https://mechneurolab.squarespace.com/)'s Mechanical Neuroimaging Lab and post-concussion injury risk modeling with [Thomas Buckley](https://sites.udel.edu/buckley-concussion-research/)'s Concussion Research Laboratory. Ongoing projects include early prediction of cognitive decline with the [Delaware Center for Cognitive Aging Research](https://sites.udel.edu/memory-research/) and interpretable EEG biomarkers for epilepsy with [Nemours Children's Health](https://www.nemours.org/).

Before my Ph.D., I earned an M.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Delaware with a thesis on synergistic human-machine prediction, and a B.S. in Electronics Engineering from the Universidad Mayor de San Andrés in La Paz, Bolivia.
